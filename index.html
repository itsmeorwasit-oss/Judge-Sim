📖 Courtroom Manual & Regulations

🏛️ What is a Statute?
A Statute is a specific legal code or law number (e.g., Statute 101) that categorizes a crime. In this game, every punishment button belongs to a specific statute category. You must memorize or look up these numbers in your Law Codex to defend your sentences if a higher court challenges your ruling.
⚖️ Objective
Manage your daily docket, survive the chaotic courtroom timelines, avoid mistrials, and maximize your metrics through strategic judicial outcomes.
📜 Evidence Flow & Objections
Click Proceed with Evidence to unpack arguments. If counseling yells OBJECTION!, check the details and choose Sustained or Overruled to maintain combo tiers and keep the peace.
🔍 Sniffer Scanner
Use the Sniffer Lab Scan tool during trial proceedings. It pauses the countdown timer and unlocks structural hardware analysis and background cash profiles of the case objects.
🕊️ Witness Interactions
Witnesses can exhibit random traits. Nervous witnesses will stall unless calmed down or threatened with your gavel. Over-talkative witnesses block your text lines until you cut them off.
🚨 Appellate Challenges & System Crises
Filing complex sentences can cause higher judges to audit your decision. Match the correct legal code's Statute Number fast to win the appeal, or face critical clout penalties. If your Jury runs out of patience entirely, be ready to pay off tracking services or force order.
👼 Guilt vs. True Innocence
Not everyone is guilty. Carefully inspect items and arguments to determine if a defendant is being framed via identity theft or deepfakes. Clearing an innocent person rewards your Integrity Meter, while wrongful convictions penalize it heavily.
